Corvus Music Player

An offline music player for iPhone and iPad

Play the music you already own. No subscription, no account, no tracking. Import your files and everything works offline.

What it does

  • Gapless playback and crossfade, built on a dual-player engine
  • Synced lyrics, with per-song timing adjustment
  • Parametric equalizer and ReplayGain volume levelling
  • Smart playlists you build from rules, plus a tag editor and duplicate finder
  • Import over Wi-Fi from a browser, from Files, or from a network server
  • CarPlay, home screen widgets, Live Activity and Siri shortcuts
  • Listening statistics, play history, favorites and bookmarks
