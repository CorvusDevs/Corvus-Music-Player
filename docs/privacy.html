Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 July 2026

Corvus Music Player is an offline music player. It plays music files you already own, stored on your own device.

What we collect

Nothing. We do not collect, store, transmit or sell any personal information. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs of any kind.

Your music, playlists, ratings, play counts, listening history and settings stay on your device. We never see them.

Optional features that use the internet

Three features can make network requests. All three are optional, and none of them sends anything to a server we operate.

  • Lyrics. If you fetch lyrics, the app sends the track title, artist, album and duration to LRClib, a free open lyrics database, so it can find a match. No identifier of you or your device is attached.
  • Scrobbling. If you connect a Last.fm or ListenBrainz account, the app sends the tracks you play to that service on your behalf. This happens only after you sign in, and only to the service you chose. Your credentials are held in the iOS Keychain. Disconnecting stops it immediately.
  • Wi-Fi Sharing. This runs a small web server on your device so a browser on the same network can upload songs. It is active only while you have the screen open, and traffic never leaves your local network.

Data stored on your device

The app keeps your imported audio files, a local library database, and cached album artwork inside its own storage. Deleting the app removes all of it. You can also remove everything from Settings.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
